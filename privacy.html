Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: we don't collect anything. Your data stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We have no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who we are

Spendable is made by John Hart ("we", "us"). Contact: hartwiredgames@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Everything you enter — accounts and balances, cards and spending, bills, subscriptions, debts, savings, pensions, household members, income, tax and student loan details, and your settings — is stored on your device. Spendable does not need an account, email address or sign-in.

Where it is stored

  • On your device, in the app's private storage.
  • An encrypted backup on your device, used only to recover your data on that device.
  • Your private iCloud, only if iCloud is turned on for Spendable. This uses Apple's iCloud and your Apple Account. We cannot see or access it. Apple's privacy policy covers iCloud.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites. Any crash reports you choose to share with developers through Apple's settings are provided by Apple in an anonymous form.

Purchases

The free trial and the unlock are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. Apple gives us anonymous sales totals.

Face ID and Touch ID

If you turn on App Lock, unlocking is handled entirely by your device. Biometric data never leaves it and we never receive it.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your data. There is no third party that receives it.

Your control

You can export your data, delete any item, erase everything in Setup › App Settings, turn off iCloud for Spendable, or delete the app. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side — but you're welcome to contact us with any question.

Children

Spendable isn't aimed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.

Spendable is made by John Hart in the UK. Estimates only — not financial advice.
