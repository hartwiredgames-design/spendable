Common questions

How much does it cost?
Spendable is free to try for 14 days. After that, a one-off £7.99 unlock keeps it for good. There's no subscription and nothing is charged unless you choose to unlock.

What happens when the trial ends?
Nothing is deleted. You can still open the app and see everything you've entered. Adding and editing needs the unlock.

I bought it on one device — how do I unlock another?
Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Spendable, go to Setup › Spendable and tap Restore purchases.

I have a code for Spendable — where do I enter it?
Open Spendable, go to Setup › Spendable and tap Redeem a code. You can also redeem it in the App Store app from your account page.

How do I get my data on another device?
Turn on iCloud for Spendable in your device's Settings, using the same Apple Account on every device. Your data syncs through your own private iCloud. You can check sync in Setup › App Settings › iCloud sync.

Does Spendable connect to my bank?
No. You enter balances yourself, so no bank logins are ever shared. It takes a minute or two a week.

Can I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I export my data?
Yes — Setup › App Settings › Backup & export creates a complete readable copy.
